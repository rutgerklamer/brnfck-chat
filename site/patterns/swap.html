Three moves: x goes to the scratch cell, y goes to x, the scratch cell goes to y.

There is no way round the third cell. Each move empties its source, so one of the two values needs a place to wait.

What it needs

  • One scratch cell that is zero; it is zero again at the end.
  • Any cell size for the main form. The form With a subtraction (wrapping cells), which is the one from the wiki, needs wrapping cells.
Common mistakes
  • Moving x onto y first. Then both values are mixed in one cell and cannot be separated.
  • Thinking the form without [-]-style moves is cheaper: the form With a subtraction (wrapping cells) on this page takes more plain steps whenever y is smaller than x.

What it costs
