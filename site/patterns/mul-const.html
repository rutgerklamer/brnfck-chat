Watch it run

It is the pattern move a value, but it gives more than it takes: for every 1 that leaves x, the target gets 3.

The factor is written into the program as that many plus signs. A minus sign instead gives a negative factor.

The loop is flat, so an optimising interpreter does the whole multiplication in one step, for any x.

What it needs

  • The factor is fixed when the program is written.
  • The target gets the product added. Any cell size; too large a product wraps.
  • To keep x: one scratch cell.
Common mistakes
  • Writing a large factor as hundreds of plus signs. Split it: two loops of 16 make 256.
  • Expecting x × y for two cells from this: the factor cannot come from the tape. See multiply two cells.
