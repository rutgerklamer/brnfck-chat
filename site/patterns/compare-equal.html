Two numbers are equal when their difference is zero. Subtracting is cheap (a flat loop), and "is it zero" is the one question brainfuck can ask.

So: x becomes x − y, and then a "not" (see not, and, or on flags) turns "zero" into 1 and everything else into 0.

What it needs

  • Wrapping cells (or x at least y), because x − y may go below zero on the way.
  • Short form: no scratch cell; both inputs are used up. Keeping form: two more cells.
  • The pointer ends where it started.
Common mistakes
  • Thinking the same trick gives "less than". The difference tells you whether the numbers differ, but on wrapping cells not which one is larger: see less than.
