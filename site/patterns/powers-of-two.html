Start at 1 and double it n times. Doubling is multiplying by the constant 2 (see multiply by a constant), into a scratch cell and back.

A general power is the same loop round a multiplication (see multiply two cells) instead of a doubling.

Doubling a number moves all its bits one place up. On a wrapping cell the top bit falls out. That is the idea the bit peel turns into a tool.

What it needs

  • One scratch cell for 2ⁿ, three for x to the power y. n and y are used up.
  • Any cell size. The result wraps: 2⁸ is 0 on 8-bit cells.
Common mistakes
  • Expecting 0 to the power 0 to be 0. The loop never runs, so the answer is the 1 it started with.
  • Leaving the base behind: the general form keeps a copy of x in a scratch cell and must clear it at the end. The snippet on the wiki does not.

Where it is used

  • The bit peel multiplies by a power of two to push bits out of the cell.
  • In the project: pow2 and shl in patterns.js build large powers of two from short loops.
