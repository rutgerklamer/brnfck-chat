Watch it run

The form “Two lines from the input” works on 17 cells; the named ones are “zero”, “letter”, “flag” and “scratch”. Press Play, drag the step slider forwards and back, or edit first line and second line:

The first line is stored with two empty cells after every letter. Then the second line is read one letter at a time, and each letter is taken off the stored one: a flat loop.

If anything is left, the letters differed. A flag is set to 1, and the flag is carried along from letter to letter, as the running total is in the form A running total that travels along of walk an array of records.

A line that is longer than the other leaves letters with nothing to cancel them, so different lengths are caught by the same test.

What it needs

  • Both lines end with a newline. Wrapping cells.
  • A zero cell at the start; three cells per letter of the longer line, plus four.
  • The tape ends empty. The pointer ends past the strings: where exactly depends on their length.
Common mistakes
  • Storing the two strings one after the other. Then every comparison of two letters is a walk between them. Next to each other, it is one step.
  • Counting the differences instead of setting a flag: 256 differences would wrap to "equal" on 8-bit cells.
