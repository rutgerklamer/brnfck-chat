Watch it run

The form “As a sum of odd numbers” works on 3 cells, named “x”, “counter” and “scratch”. Press Play, drag the step slider forwards and back, or change x with the slider:

A square is a sum of odd numbers: 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 = 16 = 4 × 4. The loop counts down from x and each round adds the next odd number, two for every unit still left plus one.

The other way is plain: copy x and multiply the two (see copy a value and multiply two cells).

What it needs

  • Two scratch cells (odd-number form) or three (copy form). Any cell size; too large a square wraps.
  • The pointer ends where it started.
Common mistakes
  • Expecting the square of 16 or more to fit an 8-bit cell: 16 × 16 = 256 wraps to 0.
