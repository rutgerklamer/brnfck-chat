Watch it run

The form “Flag next to x” works on 4 cells, named “x”, “flag”, “zero” and “out”. Press Play, drag the step slider forwards and back, or change x with the slider:

A loop runs when a cell is not zero. For the opposite we need a second cell, a flag, that starts at 1.

If x is not zero, a loop on x knocks the flag down to 0. Then a loop on the flag runs the body: it runs exactly when x was zero.

In the main form the flag sits right next to x, and the two loops are arranged so that the pointer ends on the same cell both ways.

What it needs

  • Main form: the two cells to the right of x must be zero. They are zero again at the end, and x is kept.
  • The body starts and ends on the flag cell.
  • Any cell size.
Common mistakes
  • Putting the flag anywhere else than directly right of x: the moves >-]> depend on it.
  • A body that leaves the pointer on another cell.
