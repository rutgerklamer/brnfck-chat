Watch it run

The form “The whole input” works on 10 cells; the named ones are “zero”, “letter” and “end”. Press Play, drag the step slider forwards and back, or edit the input:

After the reading loop (see read a line into memory), the pointer stands just past the last letter. One step left is the last letter.

[.<] prints the cell and steps left, until it meets the zero cell before the string. Reversing costs nothing extra: it is the direction the pointer has to walk anyway.

What it needs

  • A zero cell before the string (the read loop starts on cell 1 for that reason).
  • Everything else as for read a line into memory: wrapping cells for the newline form, 0 at the end of the input for the other.
Common mistakes
  • Text in more than one byte per letter (é, 你): the bytes of each letter come out in the wrong order. The tape holds bytes, not letters.
