Not: start a helper at 1 and take x off it. And: only if x is set does y get moved into the answer. Or: add the two and turn "anything but zero" into 1.

The short forms trust that the cells hold 0 or 1 only. The "any value" forms first turn every non-zero value into a clean 1; they cost a little more.

What it needs

  • Short forms: the inputs must be 0 or 1.
  • One scratch cell (not) or an answer cell that is zero (and).
  • Any cell size. The inputs are used up.
Common mistakes
  • Feeding a number such as 2 to a short form: "not 2" gives 255 on 8-bit cells, not 0.
  • The short "or" adds the two inputs. With arbitrary numbers the sum can wrap to exactly zero (128 + 128 on 8-bit cells) and the answer is wrong. Use the "any value" form.
