A loop tests one cell, at its start and again at its end. A "while" works the condition out into that cell before the loop, and works it out again as the last thing in the body.

Here the condition is "the letter just read is not a newline". Taking 10 off the letter makes a newline zero, and zero is what the loop can see.

What it needs

  • The condition must be in the cell under the pointer when the body ends.
  • First form: the input must contain a newline, and the cells must wrap (0 − 10 has to be a number).
  • Any cell size that wraps.
Common mistakes
  • Working out the condition only once, before the loop. The closing bracket then tests an old value.
  • Input that ends without a newline: with "0 at the end of input" the first form never sees a zero and reads for ever. The stepper adds the newline for you.
