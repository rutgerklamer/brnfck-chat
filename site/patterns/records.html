Watch it run

Brainfuck moves are relative: "two cells to the right of here". So a piece of program written for one record works on any record, if the pointer stands on that record's first cell.

Every record starts with a flag that is 1. The loop does the job, steps one record to the right and tests the next flag. The first empty flag ends the walk.

A zero cell before the first record lets [<<<] walk home again.

What it needs

  • All records the same length; here 3 cells: flag, a, b.
  • A zero flag after the last record and a zero cell one record length before the first.
  • Any cell size.
Common mistakes
  • A job that ends on another cell of the record than it started on. The step to the next record then lands in the wrong column.
  • A zero in the flag column inside the row: the walk stops there.
