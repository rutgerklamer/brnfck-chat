The counter is the loop cell. Each round takes 1 off; when it reaches zero the loop ends.

The counter is used up. To keep it, each round also adds 1 to a scratch cell, and one move at the end puts it back (see move a value).

The body may read the counter too, as long as it leaves it where it was: the form The body reads the counter adds the counter itself to a total each round.

What it needs

  • The body must return to the counter cell.
  • Any cell size. A count of zero runs the body zero times.
Common mistakes
  • Taking 1 off at the start of the round in one place and at the end in another. Both work; mixing them up inside one body does not.
  • A body that is not "flat" (it prints, or holds a loop): the optimiser cannot fold the loop, so the cost is per round. See the cost page.
