Adding is the pattern move a value: every unit that leaves y arrives in x. Subtracting is the same loop with a − at the target.

To keep y, the loop also feeds a scratch cell and a second loop moves that back, as in copy a value.

Nothing here looks at the size of the numbers. If the sum does not fit, the cell wraps round; if the difference is negative, it wraps the other way. That is the right answer in the arithmetic of the cell.

What it needs

  • Any cell size. The "keep" forms need one scratch cell that is zero.
  • The pointer ends where it started.
  • A negative result is only meaningful on wrapping cells, read as a signed number.
Common mistakes
  • Expecting subtraction to stop at zero. It does not: 3 − 5 is 254 on 8-bit cells. For a floor at zero use the saturating subtract.
  • Looping on x and adding to y when you wanted the sum in x.
