Watch it run

The form “Counting both down” works on 4 cells, named “x”, “y”, “answer” and “zero”. Press Play, drag the step slider forwards and back, or change x and y with the sliders:

Brainfuck cannot see which of two numbers is larger. It can only count both down together and see which one runs out first.

Each round takes 1 from x. Then an if and else on y: if y still has something, take 1 from it; if not, x was larger, so clear x to stop.

When the loop ends, y holds what was left: y − x, but never below zero. x was smaller exactly when something is left.

Why this is costly. The loop has a decision inside, so it is not flat, and no optimiser can fold it. The cost grows with the smaller of the two numbers. On 32-bit cells that can be billions of rounds.

What it needs

  • Main form: two zero cells right of y. Both inputs are used up. Any cell size, wrapping or not.
  • The numbers are read as unsigned (0 and up).
  • The "by bits" form needs wrapping cells of a known width and the project generators.
Common mistakes
  • Subtracting and looking at the result. On wrapping cells 3 − 5 is 254: not zero, and not visibly negative either.
  • Using the balanced-loops form on large numbers: its cost grows with x × y.
