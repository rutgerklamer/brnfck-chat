Watch it run

x × y is "add y, x times". The outer loop counts x down. Each round copies y into the answer (see copy a value).

It is a loop inside a loop. The inner loops are flat and cost one optimised step each, but the outer loop is not flat: its cost is a few steps for every unit of x.

That is fine for small numbers and hopeless for large ones. And a negative x on a wrapping cell is a very large number.

What it needs

  • Main form: two scratch cells that are zero. y is kept, x is replaced by the product.
  • Any cell size. Too large a product wraps.
  • For numbers that may be large or negative, see signed multiplication by shift-and-add.
Common mistakes
  • Looping on the larger number. The cost follows the outer counter: put the smaller one there.
  • Forgetting that −3 on 32-bit cells means 4,294,967,293 rounds.
