Notice that after the first step cell 0 held a number, the number of your letter, and that the second step printed the letter again. A small a is 97, as in the chart of Lesson 3.

The first command, the comma ,, takes the next character of the text and puts its number in the cell under the pointer. Whatever the cell held before is gone. The text a program is given to read is its input; on these pages you type all of it before the program starts. The comma was the eighth and last command of brainfuck.

Until there is nothing left

This program reads a character, then loops: it prints the character and reads the next. The input is the word hey. Press Step until the program ends, and watch what the last comma does. The line under the tape says what each comma finds:

Notice that after the third character there was nothing left to read, so the last comma put 0 in the cell. Zero is exactly what ends a loop.

The program ,[.,] is known as cat, after an old command that copies its input. Nearly every program that reads text has this shape: read once before the loop, and read again as the last thing inside it.

What a comma does when the input has run out differs between brainfuck machines: 0 here, but some give 255 and some leave the cell as it was. Every one of the Challenges states its rule, and the Reference explains the three.

What you can do now

You can read a character with ,, calculate with it, and copy a whole text with ,[.,]. With this lesson you know all eight commands:

The eight commands of brainfuck.
CommandWhat it does
+ −Add 1 to the cell under the pointer, or take 1 off.
> <Move the pointer one cell to the right or to the left.
.Print the cell as one character.
[ ]Repeat everything between them until the cell under the pointer is zero.
,Read one character of input and put its number in the cell.

The input box sends plain characters. A letter with an accent, or an emoji, is sent as two to four numbers above 127, one per comma: that is how such characters are stored (UTF-8).

To work with what you read, you have to move it around without losing it. Lesson 7 shows how.
