        Keys

        Tab
        Go to the next control; the map is one of them. Shift + Tab goes back.
        ← ↑ ↓ →
        On the map: move the view. Page Up and Page Down move a whole screen.
        + −
        On the map: zoom in and out.
        Home
        On the map: show the whole program again (0 does the same).
        Enter
        On the map: open the program text at the middle of the view. On a place in the list: go there.
        Esc
        Close the program text, or make the panel small again on a phone.
        ← →
        On Places, Tours, Display: change the tab.

        With a mouse: drag to move, scroll to zoom, click to choose a spot, double-click to zoom in. With fingers: one finger moves, two fingers zoom, a double tap zooms in. Everything can also be done with the buttons above.

        About this map

        The brainfuck program of : characters, every one of them on this map. The first writes the model's numbers into memory; the last , the engine, does the model's sums. Far out the picture comes from stored counts of the commands; from 16 commands per pixel on, the page reads the program file itself. measured

        Inside the program walks through what the parts do. Questions and caveats and credits are on the other pages. The four models (SmolLM2-135M-Instruct by Hugging Face, Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct by Alibaba Cloud, Qwen3.5-0.8B by the Qwen team of Alibaba Cloud, all Apache 2.0) are only run by brainfuck; their answers can be wrong. The DeltaNet layers of Qwen3.5-0.8B are explained on Compare the models.
