Lab

What else the programs can do.

  • Trained in brainfuckThree brainfuck programs that train a neural network themselves, from random weights, on a list of names, and print a second program that makes up new names. Train one in your browser, watch the loss fall and the weights form, see the program to scale and where its attention looks.
  • Looking at a picturebrnfck-qwen3.5-0.8b-vision: one brainfuck program that reads a picture and a question. Where every square of the picture looks, layer by layer, and what the program said about four photos.
  • Brainfuck that writes brainfuckbrnfck-qwen2.5-coder-0.5b: a brainfuck program that answers a task in words with a brainfuck program of its own. What it wrote for the 51 challenges and 60 blind tasks, every answer run on its test cases, how its model was taught and where it goes wrong.
